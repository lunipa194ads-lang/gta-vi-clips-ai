GTA VI CLIPS AI
Livestream highlights, simplified

Turn great livestream moments into clips.

GTA VI Clips AI is a desktop project designed to help creators identify engaging moments from Twitch livestreams and prepare them as vertical videos for short-form platforms.

Creator-focused video workflow

From livestream to ready-to-review video

01 / DETECT

Find engaging moments

Analyze livestream signals and chat activity to help identify moments that may be worth turning into clips.

02 / EDIT

Format for vertical video

Prepare gameplay clips in a vertical layout, with creator-selected framing and synchronized subtitles.

03 / REVIEW & PUBLISH

Keep creators in control

Review the finished video before sharing. Where enabled and approved, the project is designed to use TikTok's official authorization and content-posting APIs.

Account connection and content control

When TikTok account connection is enabled, users authorize access through TikTok's official Login Kit. The project is designed to request only the permissions needed for its enabled features. Users choose which videos to submit and remain responsible for reviewing their content and account activity.
