Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026
This policy describes how the GTA VI Clips AI desktop project is designed to handle information when you use the application and connect third-party services.

1. Who operates the application

GTA VI Clips AI is an independent desktop software project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok, Twitch, Rockstar Games, or Take-Two Interactive. Privacy questions can be sent to lunipa194ads@gmail.com.

2. Information the application may access

Depending on the features you choose to use, the application may process:

  • Twitch livestream and clip information needed to identify and prepare clips;
  • livestream audio and video, and chat messages or activity used by enabled analysis features;
  • video and audio files you select or create, including generated subtitles and editing settings;
  • TikTok account information made available through the permissions you authorize, such as basic profile information;
  • authorization tokens and technical information needed to maintain a connection to supported services.

3. How information is used

Information is used to operate the desktop workflow: identify candidate moments, process selected media, create vertical clips and subtitles, connect to services you authorize, and submit content when you explicitly use a publishing feature. The application is not designed to sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

4. Local processing and storage

The project is designed to perform video and audio processing on the user's computer. Media files, configuration, and authorization data may be stored locally as part of the application workflow. Protect your computer and local project files from access by others. Do not share access tokens, client secrets, or configuration files containing credentials.

5. Third-party services and sharing

When you connect Twitch or TikTok, information is exchanged with the relevant service as needed to provide the feature you requested. If you choose to publish a video, the video and related publishing details are sent to TikTok through its official API. The third-party service then handles that information under its own privacy policy and settings.

The project does not describe or promise any separate developer-operated cloud storage or analytics service. Any future feature that changes what information is collected or transmitted should be reflected in an updated version of this policy before it is used.

6. Retention and deletion

Media, logs, settings, and authorization data may remain in local project folders until you delete them. You can remove local files you no longer need. You can also revoke the application's access through the connected third-party service's account settings. Revoking access may prevent connected features from working.

7. Security

Use a trusted device, keep the operating system and application dependencies updated, and do not disclose credentials or tokens. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and the project cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

The application is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the connected services under their applicable age requirements. Do not use the application in a way that violates those requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the project develops. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page.
